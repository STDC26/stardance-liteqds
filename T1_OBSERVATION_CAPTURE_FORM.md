# T1 · Observation Capture Form

**UAT track:** U1-S1-T1 — First-Look Interpretation
**One completed copy per reviewer.** Filed as raw evidence; feeds
`T1_EVIDENCE_NORMALIZATION.md`.

> Completed by the observer **during and immediately after** the session. Every
> field below is blank by construction — CC does not record operator data.
> Record what was observed and what the reviewer said, verbatim where the form
> asks for it. Do not interpret, score, or adjudicate on this form;
> interpretation happens in normalization, adjudication in the matrix.

one; always add a verbatim/observed note. These are observations, not verdicts.
